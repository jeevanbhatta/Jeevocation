# Jeevocation - Vocational Training to Skilled Employment

A matching platform connecting young people (20-24) from rural areas with SMEs who need skilled workers and are willing to train. Built to address India's ~150 million skilled worker shortage.

## Problem Statement

Young people from rural areas who have finished some schooling and are enrolled or plan to enroll in vocational school need a stable vocational school-to-job conversion. Current challenges:

- Industry demand outweighs skilled workers
- Curriculum taught in school is often not industry-relevant

## Solution

Jeevocation matches SMEs who need skilled workers with youth willing to learn. We offer:

- **Industry-aligned apprenticeship** with salary
- **Clear path to employment** post-training
- **Train-to-hire** model that benefits both companies and workers

## Industries with Highest Demand

| Sector | Worker Shortage |
|--------|-----------------|
| Auto & Auto Components | 35M |
| Construction | 33M |
| Textiles & Clothing | 26M |
| Transport & Logistics | 18M |
| Retail | 17M |
| Healthcare | 13M |

## Features

- **WhatsApp-style Chatbot**: Mock chat for user signup and profile collection
- **Recommendation System**: Matches company postings with user's chat/profile data
- **Multilingual**: English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati
- **Company Postings**: Browse apprenticeship opportunities across high-demand industries
